PMA Mature Color Update

Palette:
- Dark mode: charcoal/plum base, warm slate-plum surfaces, copper accent, sage/gold secondary accents.
- Light mode: warm ivory/taupe base, plum surfaces/text, copper accent, sage/gold secondary accents.
- Trading states remain distinct: BUY green, SELL red, NEUTRAL gold.

The update is intentionally restrained to a small mature palette rather than many unrelated colors.
