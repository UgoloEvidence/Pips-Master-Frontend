# Chart Pattern / Multi-Timeframe Rebuild

Added to the PMA scanner and Day Trade Monitor:

- Falling wedge / rising wedge
- Contracting triangle
- Bull flag / bear flag
- Double top / double bottom
- Head & shoulders / inverse head & shoulders
- Breakout / breakdown retest
- Failed break / bull trap / bear trap
- Range / consolidation

Patterns are detected on the selected symbol and selected trigger timeframe. They are displayed in both Signals and Day Trade. The Day Trade Monitor supports 1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h and 1d.

Important: chart patterns are confluence, not standalone trade triggers. The existing PMA gates remain in place: pullback/zone, rejection, closed confirmation candle, freshness/no-late-entry protection, multi-timeframe context and risk checks.

Expanded Forex symbols include EURCAD, GBPCAD, AUDCAD, AUDNZD, NZDCAD, CHFJPY, CADCHF, GBPNZD and EURNZD.

Community rebuild remains: permanent messages by default, optional Admin-controlled 24-hour temporary messages with per-message countdown, member profiles, message requests, member-to-member private chat after acceptance, and Admin-to-member chat without a request. Members cannot message or request the Admin.
