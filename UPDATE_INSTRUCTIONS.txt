PMA UPDATE — LOGIN, MARKET SPEED, COMMUNITY, TRADING HISTORY

This build updates the existing Pips Master Academy project.

Main fixes:
- Login no longer displays "Connecting to PMA".
- Login timeout is more tolerant of backend wake-up time.
- Multiple devices remain independently logged in; one device does not invalidate another.
- Scanner requests the selected symbol first so switching timeframe/symbol can render the MTF context without waiting for an 8-symbol batch.
- Backend market-data request/cache timing was increased to avoid premature frontend timeouts.
- Day Trade keeps cached verified snapshots and prefetches other market categories in the background.
- Indices, Metals, Crypto and Commodities use the same verified TradingView/Yahoo fallback path; no fake prices are generated.
- Added full Trading History section to the hamburger menu.
- Trading History reads backend trade_history and signal_history data.
- History retention defaults to 7 days and the administrator can turn automatic 7-day deletion off/on.
- Community shows member list, community bio, reply-to messages, media, member/admin delete controls, and 7-day disappearing-message setting.
- Administrator cannot leave the community.
- Regular approved members can leave the community; unapproved members can request access.
- Admin can approve/remove/suspend/restore members and change community name, bio, picture and disappearing-message setting.

Important:
- This package does not invent market prices or historical trades.
- Full live provider speed still depends on TradingView/Yahoo and Render response time. The app now caches verified results and avoids failing the UI too early.
