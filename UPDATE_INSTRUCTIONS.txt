PMA targeted update: Trading History analytics, faster Day Trade switching, and email verification.

EMAIL VERIFICATION SETUP (required for new registrations):
Use either Resend or SMTP on the backend.

Resend:
PMA_EMAIL_FROM=verified@yourdomain.com
RESEND_API_KEY=your_resend_api_key

OR SMTP:
PMA_EMAIL_FROM=your_verified_sender@example.com
SMTP_HOST=smtp.example.com
SMTP_PORT=587
SMTP_USER=your_smtp_username
SMTP_PASSWORD=your_smtp_password
SMTP_TLS=1

PMA_EMAIL_VERIFY_TTL=900

Existing accounts are treated as already verified during migration so the update does not lock existing users out. New accounts must enter the 6-digit code emailed to them before login.
