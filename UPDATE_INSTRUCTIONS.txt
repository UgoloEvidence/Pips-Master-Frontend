## Direction-mapping / false-ready fix
- The scanner no longer treats a bullish/bearish trend alone as a confirmed BUY/SELL.
- The selected trigger timeframe is now the actual trigger source.
- BUY requires bullish trigger-timeframe trend, RSI confirmation, bullish recent structure and multi-timeframe support; it remains APPROACHING until price closes above the prior five-candle high.
- SELL requires the mirrored conditions and remains APPROACHING until price closes below the prior five-candle low.
- The previous trigger calculation incorrectly included the current candle in the five-candle high/low, which could make confirmation logically inconsistent. The trigger now uses the five completed candles immediately before the current candle.
- Entry, SL and TP are calculated from the confirmed setup and the frontend freezes those levels once TRIGGERED/ACTIVE. Polling cannot move active levels unless the setup direction changes.
- Stop loss uses recent structure plus a small ATR buffer rather than a 60-candle extreme. This is a tighter structure-aware stop, not a guarantee against stop-outs.
- Historical validation now tests the same breakout/breakdown trigger concept and reports samples/wins/losses/win rate. A historical result is not a guarantee of future performance.
- The UI shows WATCH BUY/WATCH SELL while a setup is only approaching; it shows BUY/SELL only after confirmation.

HOTFIX 2026-09-22 — HIGH-PRECISION SCANNER FILTER
- Tightened automated scanner directional gate to reduce marginal setups.
- BUY forecasts require bullish trigger structure, at least 3/5 MTF bullish contexts, RSI 52–68, and TradingView technical rating >= +0.25.
- SELL forecasts require bearish trigger structure, at least 3/5 MTF bearish contexts, RSI 32–48, and TradingView technical rating <= -0.25.
- Zone rejection forecasts are retained but require stronger exhaustion conditions: RSI >=60 near supply for pullback forecasts, or RSI <=40 near demand for bounce forecasts.
- Marginal signals are now rejected as NO TRADE instead of being surfaced merely because a directional bias exists.
- Internal setup score remains available for ranking, while frontend continues to display qualitative strength labels rather than a 100/100 confidence percentage.
- This is a precision filter, not a guarantee of higher future win rate. Actual win-rate improvement must be measured with out-of-sample backtesting using the same entry/exit rules.
